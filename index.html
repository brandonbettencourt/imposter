How to play

  1. Pass the phone: Each player privately taps to reveal their secret word, then hides it before passing on.
  2. Give clues (2 rounds): Go around twice. On your turn, say one word related to your secret word — never say the word itself.
  3. Talk & vote: Debate whose clues felt off, point on 3-2-1 to vote in person, then reveal the answer!
